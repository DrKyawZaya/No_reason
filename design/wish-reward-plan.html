Wish cards as a reward

Plan for changing the wish card in 1.1.0. 1.1.0 is not on Play yet, so we can replace the current editor before anyone uses it.

The idea in one line

Each finished ကိုးနဝင်း stage earns one wish card. You make the wish once, for yourself or for someone. After you make it, it is sealed: it can't be edited, only looked at and shared.

1 · Earning the card

  • Finishing a stage earns one card for that stage. The full 81 days give 9 cards. If you start ကိုးနဝင်း again, you can earn 9 more.
  • The "သာဓု" screen shows ဆုတောင်းကတ် ရပါပြီ with a button to make the wish now.
  • If you close it without making the wish, the card waits for you. Home shows a small banner, ဆုတောင်းကတ် ၁ ခု ရှိပါသည် ›, until you use it. It never expires.
  • Free counting and your own ပုတီး don't earn cards. Only ကိုးနဝင်း stages do, so the card stays special.

Confirm before sealing
  1. For whom: ကိုယ်တိုင် (myself) or တစ်ယောက်ယောက် (someone). For someone there is a name box with quick choices: အမေ, အဖေ, မိဘ, ချစ်သူ, or type any name.
  2. Words: pick a ready-made wish or write your own, as now. The wish you wrote at the start of that stage is offered first.
  3. Picture: pick a background.
  4. Seal: tap ဆုတောင်းမည်. A confirm box says it can't be changed afterwards. After confirming, the card is saved with its date and becomes read-only.

3 · After sealing

  • The card shows a small mark, ဆုတောင်းပြီး · ၂၀၂၆ အောက်တိုဘာ ၂၈, so it is clear it was made once on that day.
  • Only two buttons: မျှဝေမည် (share to Facebook, Viber, Messenger…) and ဓာတ်ပုံ သိမ်းမည် (save to Photos).
  • No edit button. The words, name and picture stay as they were made.

How it's stored

  • Each sealed card is saved on the phone with: stage, which round of ကိုးနဝင်း, for whom (myself, or the name), the words, the picture and the date.
  • The card is drawn again from that saved data whenever you open or share it, so it always looks the same.
  • Still no internet, no account and no new permissions. Uninstalling the app removes the cards, as with all progress.

What I need you to decide

  1. Sharing: share a sealed card as many times as you like (my suggestion, so you can send it to several people and apps), or only once?
  2. Card for yourself: show ကိုယ်တိုင်အတွက် on the card, or no name line at all?
  3. Name chips: are အမေ, အဖေ, မိဘ, ချစ်သူ right? Add or change any (for example ဆရာ, သားသမီး, မိသားစု).
  4. Two wishes per stage: the app already asks for a wish at the start of each stage. Keep both (start wish is private, card wish is shared), or drop the start wish and use only the card?
  5. Ready-made wishes: separate lists for myself and for someone, for example ကျန်းမာချမ်းသာပါစေ for both and ဤကုသိုလ်ကို အမျှဝေပါသည် only for someone? Please send the wording.
  6. The 81-day finish: make the 9th card look extra special, for example with a gold border and "ကိုးနဝင်း ပြည့်စုံ"?

Work, once you decide

  1. Save sealed cards and earned rewards on the phone, with tests.
  2. Rework the card screen into "make" (once) and "view and share" (sealed).
  3. Add the Home banner for a card waiting to be made.
  4. Add the collection to the ဇယား tab.
  5. Screenshots of every new screen for you to check, then a test APK.
