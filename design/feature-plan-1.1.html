Where the pictures come from

This is the biggest choice in the plan. Three ways:

WayGoodWatch out
A. Drawn by the app's own code RecommendedAs above: gradients and silhouettes. Tiny (almost no size added), sharp on every screen, no copyright question, and no AI label needed in Play.Stylised, not a real photograph. It will not look like a famous pagoda photo.
B. Real photos you supplyReal places that mean something to you.Only photos you took, or ones whose licence allows apps. Each adds about 150 to 300 KB. Photos of famous buildings taken by others are not free to use.
C. AI-generated picturesFast and varied.Play's AI declaration then has to be answered "labelled". Quality of Buddha images needs care, because a wrong-looking image can offend.

Suggestion: start with way A for 1.1.0. Later, add your own photo as background from the gallery (no storage permission is needed). That gives every user their own favourite pagoda without the app carrying copyrighted pictures.

Rules for every theme

  • A darkened layer sits behind the counting area so beads and numbers stay easy to read.
  • The pictures are still. Nothing moves while you count.
  • Respect for the Buddha image: the seated Buddha is a calm silhouette, shown above the beads, and never behind a button or text.

How it works

  • The card is drawn in the app and sent as a picture. Pictures show the Myanmar text correctly for everyone. Plain text can turn into garbage on phones that still use Zawgyi.
  • Sharing uses the phone's own share menu. The app needs no new permissions and no internet.
  • Saving to the gallery works on Android 10 and later without any permission. On older phones only Share is shown.
  • The wish and name stay on the phone. Nothing is uploaded, so the Play Data safety answer stays "no data collected".
  • The footer shows the app name and Play Store link, so people who see the card can find the app. You can turn this off if you prefer.
  • To make the text look the same on every phone, I would include the Padauk font (free licence) for the card, about 0.5 to 1 MB.
